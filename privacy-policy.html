Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of personal information in Estmation, a multiplayer card game published under the Golden Blue Rooms name. The game is currently in development. Features and data practices described below must be verified before public release.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, Estmation may process account identifiers, player names, profile information, authentication information supplied through identity providers, game statistics, match history, experience points (XP), levels, and multiplayer session information.

Technical information may also be processed by service providers to operate authentication, networking, security, and cloud features.

2. How Information May Be Used

  • To create and maintain player accounts.
  • To authenticate players and restore sessions.
  • To support online multiplayer functionality.
  • To maintain player progress and game statistics.
  • To detect errors, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the game.

3. Authentication

Estmation is being developed with Unity authentication services and Unity Player Accounts. Supported sign-in methods may change before release.

Authentication providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Estmation's specific authentication flows and account-linking behavior will be verified before launch.

4. Multiplayer and Cloud Services

Estmation is designed to support public and private multiplayer tables, game sessions, player profiles, and synchronized progression.

Unity Gaming Services is used during development. The final services, hosting arrangements, and data transfers will be reviewed and disclosed before public release.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the game, including authentication, multiplayer, and cloud-service providers. The final list of providers and the information shared with each will be confirmed before launch.

6. Data Retention and Security

We intend to retain personal information only as necessary for legitimate game operations and applicable legal requirements, and to use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Specific retention periods and deletion procedures have not yet been finalized.

7. Player Rights and Account Deletion

Players may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law. A contact method and verified account-deletion procedure will be published before launch.

8. Children's Privacy

Estmation's intended age audience and any age-related privacy protections are still being determined. Appropriate requirements will be implemented before public release.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the game's features and services are finalized. Material changes will be reflected in the published version.

10. Contact

The official Golden Blue Rooms privacy contact address is being established and will be added before public release.
